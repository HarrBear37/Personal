A Day in Behrend Fields

For this series of photos, I want them to feel like a day in Behrend Fields. It will be a quiet journey that empathizes the great parts of the fields.

The photos I took during the day show the open, peaceful side of Behrend Fields. The sun's beams bring an alive and welcoming feeling.

I feel as though this is a great establishing shot. It shows the location while maintaining a welcoming atmosphere. I enjoy passing it during my bike rides.

I enjoy passing this bridge on my bike. The trees are beautiful, and the sound of the flowing water is calming. Crossing over to the fields is always enjoyable experience.

As night comes, it brings a sense of danger. The places we see in the day are threatening at night. There is comfort in the dark - a feeling everyone can relate to.

Under different lighting and a change of view, the bridge becomes ominous. Adding to it is the lens flare, and the dead lights add to the mystery. A different perspective brings different feelings.

Hope you enjoyed!
